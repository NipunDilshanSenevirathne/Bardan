Section 5. Thermal Work Limit zones

Clause 5.2. Where the Thermal Work Limit is below 115 W/m2, ordinary work shall not continue; only essential maintenance or rescue work may be carried out, and workers shall rehydrate at 1.5 litres per hour.

Clause 5.3. Where the Thermal Work Limit is from 115 to 140 W/m2, shade and ventilation shall be provided, no employee shall work alone, and workers shall rehydrate at 1.2 litres per hour.

Clause 5.4. Where the Thermal Work Limit is from 140 to 220 W/m2, acclimatized workers shall not work alone, and workers shall rehydrate at 1.0 litre per hour.

Clause 5.5. Where the Thermal Work Limit is above 220 W/m2, there are no thermal stress limits, and workers shall rehydrate at 0.5 litres per hour.

Section 6. Definitions

Clause 6.1. An unacclimatized employee means a new employee, or an employee who has been off work for more than 14 days through illness or leave.
