How AI is integrated · Bardan Intelligence

AI proposes. Engines decide. People approve.

Every model call passes a safety router before it and the Guardian after it. Nothing a model writes can decide an emergency, relax a limit, diagnose, rank a worker or act without the named person’s approval.

1What comes in
  • A voice clip or typed report, English or Arabic (or both)
  • A worker’s question, an HSE question
  • A lab report image or PDF
  • A regulation excerpt to encode
  • Tomorrow’s plan, to explain
2Safety routerbefore any model
  • Urgent words open the emergency card, with no model and no network.
  • Kidney, diagnosis and medicine questions get approved messages; a model may only pick one.
  • Names, worker IDs and clinical pseudonyms never enter a prompt.
  • Real data on a free tier: every AI call is refused.
3One AI adapterGemini API, free tier, optional
  • Six tiers: fast, audio, reasoning, bulk, embedding, speech.
  • A measured fallback order per tier; a busy or used-up model is skipped.
  • Time budgets of 8–20 s; a timeout ends the try at once.
  • The interface only ever says “Bardan Intelligence”.

5What the person sees
  • Passes: shown, with a trace of every check.
  • Blocked: an approved message in the person’s language instead.
  • The blocked draft is kept in the trace for review, never shown.
  • Reports: the heard words are shown back, and nothing is saved until the worker confirms.

  • Deterministic engines decide: the rule engine, the planner and the task ledger.
