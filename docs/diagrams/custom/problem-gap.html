The problem

The heat rules and warnings exist. The step after the warning is what fails.

Already in place

Heat rules and the 12:30–15:00 midday break

Forecasts and heat warnings

Checklists signed off by supervisors

Clinics and kidney tests for those who reach them

Where it breaks

The water point is empty, but the sheet says “done”

The shade is full at break time

A new worker is pushed too hard in his first days

An abnormal kidney test never reaches a doctor

What Bardan adds

Heat-safe work planned the evening before

Each rule becomes a task with an owner, a deadline and proof

“This didn’t happen” without being named

Kidney care kept private and continuous

Bardan does not replace the rules. It makes sure the protection they require happens, and can show that it did.
