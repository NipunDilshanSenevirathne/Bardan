How Bardan works

One loop, from tomorrow’s forecast
to proof it was done.

1
Foresee18:00, the evening beforeEngine

A 72-hour heat forecast for each zone (Liljegren WBGT) with a 90% interval. Planning uses the cautious upper end.

2
Planthe same eveningSupervisor

An optimiser (CP-SAT) moves heavy work out of the worst hours, fits breaks to the 20 shaded seats and keeps new workers within acclimatization. The rule engine re-checks it; a named supervisor approves.

3
Live dayfrom 07:00Worker

Live readings govern: a harsher reading tightens the plan at once; a stale one is “not confirmed safe”. Each worker gets one clear action card in English or Arabic, read aloud.

4
Actwithin minutesSupervisorHSE

Every required action becomes a task with an owner, a start-by time and the evidence it needs. A task nobody accepts in time escalates to HSE.

5
Proveall day, and afterHSEReviewer

Verified only with the required evidence and no open dispute. The compliance pack checks itself, and every step is kept in a hash-chained audit log.

Each evening the loop starts again, with the week’s fairness in view.

When a worker presses “This didn’t happen”

  1. The task reopens and goes to HSE.
  2. The supervisor never sees who said it.
  3. HSE closes it only with its own check, made after the dispute, and a written finding.
  4. The worker is told what was checked and can challenge again.

When a kidney test is abnormal

  1. Lab import checks identity and units.
  2. A clinician reviews it; the referral cannot close silently.
  3. The employer sees only “clinic visit 14:00–16:00”.
  4. The worker keeps a Kidney Passport and sees who opened the record.

When the phone has no network

  1. The emergency card still opens, with AI off too.
  2. Reports and alerts wait on the phone, even through a reload.
  3. “Received by the server” and “seen by the site team” are shown separately, only when true.
